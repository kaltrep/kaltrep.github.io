Identity Overview

Company

Kaltrep is a three-person technical consulting firm specializing in Data Engineering, Data Science, Analytics, Business Process Optimization, and System Integrations. The team brings operational depth — they have held senior data leadership roles, built enterprise Lakehouse architectures, and managed large-scale pipeline infrastructure.

Positioning

Operators, not just advisors. Kaltrep delivers practical outcomes — working systems, actionable roadmaps, and documented handoffs — not slide decks. They bridge the gap between executive strategy and technical execution. A subsidiary of Data Driven Investments.

Brand Personality

  • Precise and technically credible — never vague or buzzword-heavy
  • Confident without arrogance — the work speaks for itself
  • Warm but professional — approachable expertise, not cold consulting-speak
  • Premium and restrained — small firm that charges accordingly

Voice & Tone

Precise

Name the technology, the outcome, the metric. No hand-waving. Specificity signals expertise.

"We cut pipeline costs 40% by migrating to a Lakehouse architecture on Databricks."

Direct

Short sentences. Active voice. Lead with the outcome, not the process. Business decision-makers read fast.

"We rebuild workflows, not just recommend them."

Confident

No hedging. No "we believe" or "we hope to." State what you do and what clients get.

"Every engagement ships with runbooks, documentation, and a 30-day support window."
Tagline"Connecting data. Driving decisions." — use in footers, signatures, closing slides.
Hero tagline (web)"Turning data into direction — and complexity into competitive advantage."
Closing CTA"Let's build something exceptional together." — use on closing slides and contact sections.
Avoid"Synergy", "leverage", "holistic", "best-in-class", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "robust solution". Use specific verbs: build, ship, reduce, automate, connect, design, measure.
Tone in proposalsProfessional but not stiff. Write like a senior engineer who also understands the business — not like a management consultant.
NumbersLead with numbers where possible: "18+ years", "260K+ integrations/month", "40% cost reduction". Specificity builds trust.
Document & Slide Standards

PowerPoint / Slide Decks

  • Background: Abyss #0D1520 (dark slides) or Frost #EDF2F7 / Cloud #F0F4F9 (light slides)
  • Headings: Georgia bold, Frost on dark / Midnight on light
  • Section labels: Trebuchet MS, Ice Blue, uppercase, 9pt, 6pt letter-spacing
  • Body: Calibri 13–14pt, Glacier color
  • Accent lines & rules: Ice Blue, 1.5–2pt weight
  • Service/stat cards: Abyss card on dark slides · Midnight card on light slides, Ice Blue top border
  • Ghost mark: Large K-graph mark at low opacity (ICE, 78% transparent) used as background decoration on cover and closing slides
  • Cover structure: Section label → Title (40pt Georgia bold) → Ice rule → Client/date

Word / Proposal Documents

  • Header: "KALTREP" Georgia bold left · "Technical Consulting" Calibri right · Ice Blue bottom rule
  • Footer: "Confidential — Kaltrep Technical Consulting" left · page number right · light top rule
  • H1: Georgia bold 40pt, Midnight, Ice Blue bottom border
  • H2: Georgia bold 28pt, Midnight
  • H3: Trebuchet MS bold 22pt, Ice Blue — used for phase labels
  • Table headers: Midnight bg, Frost bold text, Trebuchet MS
  • Alternating rows: #F0F4F9 Cloud fill
  • Cover block: Full-width Midnight rectangle, Frost wordmark, ICE rule below
  • Signature lines: Midnight underline (client) · Ice Blue underline (Kaltrep rep)
  • Fonts available in Office: Georgia, Calibri, Trebuchet MS — no installation required

Email Signature

  • Name: Outfit 500 or Calibri bold, Midnight
  • Title: Trebuchet MS, Ice Blue, small caps or uppercase
  • Divider: 1px, #D0DCE8
  • Contact info: Calibri, Glacier
  • Logo: Small SVG horizontal lockup (dark text version), ~100px wide
  • No banner image, no marketing tagline in signatures
Usage Rules

✓ Do

  • Use Ice Blue only for accent moments: logo nodes, horizontal rules, hover states, key numbers
  • Use Georgia for all display headings and wordmarks
  • Use Midnight + Frost as the dominant pairing
  • Keep the K-graph nodes always Ice Blue regardless of context
  • Use solid color backgrounds only (no gradients as primary bg)
  • Lead copy with specific, verifiable outcomes and metrics
  • Use letter-spacing generously on labels and caps text
  • Keep the Outfit font at weight 300 for body text

✗ Don't

  • Use red, orange, or warm accents — the prior red brand is retired
  • Use Inter, Roboto, or system-ui as a primary display font
  • Use Ice Blue as a large background fill
  • Recolor or distort the K-graph mark
  • Place the logo on photographic or patterned backgrounds
  • Use buzzwords: synergy, leverage, holistic, cutting-edge
  • Use purple gradients or other generic AI-adjacent aesthetics
  • Mix Rajdhani (the previous font) into any new materials
